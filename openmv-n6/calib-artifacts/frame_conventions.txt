## Frames

R_ti      TI sensor frame as unpacked from TLV1: x right, y forward (boresight),
          z up. Origin = antenna reference per SDK 3.x OOB demo.
R         Project radar frame = ti_to_project(R_ti): x forward, y LEFT, z up.
          (x,y,z) = (y_fwd, -x_right, z_up). Units metres; v m/s, FOLDED
          (alias period 1.298 m/s at 3TX), positive = away from sensor.
C         Reference visible camera (PAG7936), OpenCV convention:
          x right, y down, z forward (optical axis). Image 640x400,
          NOT mirrored (verified by walk-in pixel-track vs radar-az).
T         Lepton 3.5 thermal frame. Reached ONLY by composition
          T_T<-R = T_T<-C · T_C<-R, never solved independently.

## Known empirical anomalies (measured 2026-08-09, must be resolved knowingly)

1. az/el signs are FLIPPED vs the documented project frame:
   empirically az_radar ≈ +atan((u-cx)/f); kneeling reported POSITIVE
   elevation. Consistent with a 180-rolled board or a sign error in
   ti_to_project. EXPECT the extrinsic solve to land ~180 deg from
   R_CANONICAL. When it does: fix the convention in mmwave.py
   ti_to_project with a documented commit, re-derive, and DO NOT mix
   correspondences picked before and after the fix in one solve.
2. Never rescale mmwave.py output: TLV1 floats verified bit-exact,
   range grid 0.04363 m matches profileCfg, doppler passed dr/dt physics.

## Config freeze notes (A4)

- clutterRemoval OFF (static TCR must keep returning points).
- rangeBias = 0.0 -> UNCALIBRATED. Must run measureRangeBiasAndRxChanPhase
  with the TCR at 1.5 m boresight BEFORE any correspondence collection
  that will be kept: changing the phase table moves boresight and VOIDS
  any previously solved (R,t).
- cfarFovCfg range ceiling = 5.0 m: place "5 m" holds at <= 4.7 m and
  V05 (longest reliable) at ~4.7 m. Do not edit the cfg mid-calibration.
- aoaFovCfg stays +-60/+-30 for THIS calibration (frozen); the picker's
  ghost veto (|az_radar - az_pixel| > 12 deg) handles out-of-cone ghosts.
- IF-HPF floor: nothing below 0.375 m, ever. All holds >= 1.5 m anyway.
- Transport = USB (XDS110): CLI /dev/ttyACM1, DATA /dev/ttyACM2.
  UART7 migration later requires the short re-validation of PDF sec. 11.
- cfg must be re-sent (tools/send_radar_cfg.py) after every radar power
  cycle; live.py warns after 15 s of radar silence.
